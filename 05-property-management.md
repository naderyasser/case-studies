# Property Management for Saudi Real Estate

**Kind:** the full rental lifecycle for a property company, in Arabic (RTL) with the Hijri calendar

## What it does
- **Properties** — apartments, villas, shops, warehouses and land with images, pricing and status
- **Contracts** — create, activate, renew and terminate, with generated contract numbers
- **ZATCA-compliant invoicing** — tax invoices with TLV-encoded QR codes, Arabic PDFs, Hijri dates
- **Payments** — cash, bank transfer, cheque, online or platform, partial payments included
- **Owners and tenants** — national addresses, VAT numbers, bank details
- **Dashboard** — charts, overdue tracking, payment breakdowns

`Next.js 16` `React 19` `React Hook Form + Zod` `ZATCA QR` `Arabic PDF` `Hijri calendar`
